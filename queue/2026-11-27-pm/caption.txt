MYTH: a tight stop loss means zero risk. 🛑

FACT: a stop limits your loss, it doesn't remove risk. In fast markets price can gap through your level and your exit fills lower (slippage).

Do this instead:
1️⃣ Know where you're wrong
2️⃣ Size the trade to that level
3️⃣ Risk 1% or less per trade
